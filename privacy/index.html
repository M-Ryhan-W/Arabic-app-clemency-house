Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ihya Institute ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Ihya Arabic App website and Android app (together, the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account, we collect your email address and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase) and are never stored or visible to us in plain text.

Voice recordings

Speaking exercises, pronunciation checks, and AI conversation practice ask for microphone access. Your recording is sent securely to our servers, transcribed and evaluated by an AI model, and the result (a score and written feedback) is returned to the App. Raw audio from these exercises is processed to generate that feedback and is not stored afterward.

Content you create

This includes text you type into AI-assisted lesson help or practice conversations, and anything you post to the Community feature — including written corrections and, optionally, a short voice recording attached to a post or correction.

Learning and usage data

We track lesson completions, streaks, daily activity, and similar progress data tied to your account so the App can show your progress and keep it in sync across sessions.

Log and device data

Like most online services, our hosting and infrastructure providers automatically log standard technical data (such as IP address, device/browser type, and timestamps) for security and reliability purposes.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and secure your account, and let you sign in across devices.
  • To evaluate spoken Arabic and generate pronunciation/conversation feedback using AI.
  • To track and display your lesson progress and streaks.
  • To power the Community feature, including AI-assisted and peer corrections.
  • To send account-related email, such as a welcome message or password reset.
  • To respond to problem reports you submit from within the App.
  • To detect abuse and enforce fair-use limits (e.g. daily request limits).
  • For teacher accounts specifically, to show a teacher the progress and posts of their own students.

3. AI Processing

Speech evaluation, conversation practice, lesson help, and text-to-speech audio are powered by Google Cloud AI services (Vertex AI / Gemini models and Google Cloud Text-to-Speech). Audio and text you submit for these features are sent to Google Cloud for processing in order to generate a response, subject to Google's own data handling terms as our processor.

4. Who We Share Information With

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use advertising or analytics/tracking SDKs in the App. We share information only with the service providers that help us run the App:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and backend hosting.
  • Google Cloud — AI-powered speech evaluation, conversation practice, and text-to-speech audio generation.
  • Resend — delivery of transactional emails (e.g. welcome, password reset).

These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes. We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Community Feature & Data Retention

Community posts and any corrections (including attached audio) are automatically and permanently deleted 7 days after posting. Your account and learning-progress data is retained for as long as your account is active, and deleted upon account deletion as described below.

6. Your Choices

  • You can review and update your account details from within the App.
  • You can delete your own Community posts and corrections at any time from "My Community Posts".
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us below.
  • Microphone access can be revoked at any time in your device settings; speaking exercises will not work without it.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures — including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access-controlled databases — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International Data Transfers

Our service providers operate infrastructure in multiple countries, so your information may be processed outside the country where you live.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

11. Contact Us

Questions about this policy or a data deletion request can be sent to privacy@ihyaarabicapp.com.
